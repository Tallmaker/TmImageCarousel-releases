Tm Image Carousel shows pictures from your Qlik app data.

Browse pictures in a carousel or a scrolling gallery, with captions, values and links beside each picture. App selections narrow the pictures shown, and you can let viewers select items or open a related sheet.

## Start here

- [When to use](when-to-use.md): check the fit and data requirements.
- [Getting started](getting-started.md): add your first pictures.
- [Settings](settings.md): choose fields, actions and appearance.
- [Modes](modes.md): browse with Carousel or Gallery.
- [Tips](tips.md): fix missing pictures and understand limits.

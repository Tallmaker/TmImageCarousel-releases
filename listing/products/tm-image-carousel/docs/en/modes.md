## Carousel

Choose **Carousel** in **Appearance**, **Presentation**, **Layout** when viewers should focus on one picture or a small group. It is the default layout. **Pictures per view** shows one to four tiles or fits them automatically; **Move by** chooses a single-picture or full-view step.

Click the arrows or previews, or swipe left and right with touch or a pen. Vertical movement still scrolls the sheet. With keyboard focus on a tile, Left and Right move between tiles and move the view at its edge; Home and End go to the first and last picture. Enter or Space runs the image click action, and Escape cancels an open selection.

With **Zoom on picture** On, viewers can zoom into the current picture up to 4×. Drag or use the arrow keys to move around the zoomed picture (at its edge the arrow keys move to the next or previous picture again); press 0 or Escape to return to full size. Auto-play pauses while a picture is zoomed, and the zoom resets when the picture changes.

The preview strip is a separate tab stop: use Left, Right, Home and End there. With **Auto-play** on, use **Pause auto-play** or **Resume auto-play** to control movement. The counter, preview highlight and position announcement follow the displayed picture together.

## Gallery

Choose **Gallery** to scan many pictures or select several items visually. Pictures appear in a grid that scrolls inside the object. **Tile size** controls the minimum tile width; image fit, captions and the optional value still apply. Carousel movement controls are hidden.

With **Select value**, click a tile or press Enter or Space to toggle it in the current selection. Gallery also treats **Nothing** as **Select value**. Selected tiles have a check mark and frame. Confirm or cancel with Qlik's selection toolbar; after confirmation, clear the selection to see all items again.

The grid is one tab stop. Arrow keys move between tiles; Home and End move to row ends; Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End reach the first and last picture. Page Up and Page Down move by a screenful. Type the first letters of a caption to jump to a matching loaded picture; typing resets after a short pause.

A linked tile shows an **Open link** link for the mouse; from the keyboard, focus the tile and press Shift+Enter to open it. Hover or focus a linked tile or one with shortened text to see its full caption, value and link hint. Escape dismisses the tooltip. Pictures load as you scroll, and only nearby rows are drawn. Both layouts show notices when data is incomplete or exceeds the picture limit.

## Small objects and snapshots

As space shrinks, captions, values and controls can disappear to preserve room for pictures. In Carousel, a preview strip can give way to dots. Enlarge the object if you need the text and controls.

Snapshots and stories keep the pictures in view when the snapshot was taken. They do not move or auto-play, and the picture files must remain available when the story is viewed.
